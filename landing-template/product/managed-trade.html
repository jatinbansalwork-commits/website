A licensed broker is accountable for every entry

  • First-party filing: KlearNow files on its own licences rather than handing you to a referral chain.
  • Named broker of record: You know who is accountable for your entries, and you can reach them.
  • Full audit trail: Every filing carries its documents and field-level provenance.
  • Your data stays yours: Hand off the work, not the visibility. The platform stays open to your team.

How it works

01

Scoping and SOP design. We document how your entries should be handled before we file anything. Named team assignment. A dedicated pod, not a queue.

02

Integration build. Broker, ERP, TMS, WMS connections. Standard configs included. Managed filing. KlearNow files first-party as broker of record.

03

Post-entry management. PEA audits, PSC and CAPE handled on your behalf. Reporting and review. Audit-ready archive plus a standing operating review.
