How to choose

Eight tests to run on your own trade data.

Every global trade platform quotes visibility and “one dashboard.” Few say what they measured. Run these in a pilot on your lanes before you sign anything.

  • Visibility

    2
    • Cross-broker visibilitySee clearance speed, exceptions, and fees for every broker in one dashboard—spot issues early and keep freight moving.
    • Consolidated customs dataFilings from every partner and country in one interactive view—track entries, spot exceptions, stay audit-ready without the back-and-forth.

  • Finance

    2
    • Duty & bond forecastingForecast duty spend and bond sufficiency weeks ahead with live entry data so finance secures cash and avoids clearance delays.
    • Tariff what-if modellingRun instant simulations on tariff impacts, see landed costs, and adjust sourcing or pricing before new rates hit.

  • Compliance

    2
    • Full audit trailEvery field change, correction, and filing decision traceable to source documents—not an export you assemble after the fact.
    • SOC 2 Type 2 on filing workflowsControls your security team can review on live filing paths, not a generic cloud badge on the marketing site.

  • Scale

    2
    • Licensed filing lanesUS, Canada, UK, and EU lanes on one HTS and party master—built for multinationals, forwarders, and customs brokers at high volume.
    • Pilot on your shipment mixValidate with your brokers, SKU mix, and exception rate—not a demo built on the vendor’s sample entries.
