KlearNow FAQ: product questions

Who is KlearNow for?

KlearNow serves importers, freight forwarders, customs brokers, self-filers, and trade compliance teams that need structured trade data, faster clearance, and visibility from document intake through delivery.

What are KlearData, KlearHub, and Managed Trade?

KlearData turns inbound trade documents into filing-ready data. KlearHub is the customs operations platform for entries, corrections, and tracking. Managed Trade adds licensed broker filing and port-to-door execution—you can start with one product and expand on the same data foundation.

Do I need all three products?

No. Many teams start with KlearData for intake and extraction. Add KlearHub when you need broker-grade operations software, or Managed Trade when you want licensed filing and end-to-end execution—without replacing systems later.

How does document intake work?

Send invoices, packing lists, and other trade documents by email or integration. KlearData classifies and extracts fields with traceability to source documents, then pushes clean data to filing tools, ERP, or TMS.

Does KlearNow support ABI filing and broker workflows?

Yes. KlearNow supports ABI-certified U.S. entry filing with licensed broker oversight, validation before goods move, and audit-ready records for compliance and finance teams.

What onboarding and support do you provide?

Broker-led onboarding, help migrating from spreadsheets and legacy tools, and responsive experts who understand customs operations—not generic ticket queues.

Which regions and trade lanes does KlearNow support?

KlearNow supports U.S. ABI entry filing and global trade document processing for importers and logistics teams shipping across major trade lanes. Talk to us about your origin countries, ports, and compliance requirements.

Does KlearNow integrate with our ERP or TMS?

Yes. Cleaned trade data and status updates can flow to ERP, TMS, and warehouse systems via APIs and standard integrations so operations and finance stay aligned without re-keying.

Can we keep our own customs broker?

Absolutely. Use KlearData and KlearHub with your broker, or add Managed Trade when you want licensed filing and port-to-door execution from the KlearNow team.

How is our trade data secured?

KlearNow is ISO 27001 certified and aligned with GDPR for applicable processing. Documents and entry data are handled under audited security controls from intake through filing.

How long does rollout usually take?

Timelines depend on lanes, document types, and integrations. Many teams start with a focused pilot on live shipments, then expand products and regions with broker-led onboarding.

How is KlearNow priced?

Pricing reflects products used, shipment volume, and services such as managed filing. Share your operation type and monthly volume—we will recommend the right mix of KlearData, KlearHub, and Managed Trade.
