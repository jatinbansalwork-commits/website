Compliance coverage

Depth where trade gets hard.

Anyone can file a clean consumption entry. KlearNow is built for the regulations that keep compliance teams up at night, with licensed brokers and domain logic behind every one.

Up to 70% time savings dedicated to accountability and rights management

Day after day, our customers gain in efficiency thanks to our services, your time is our priority.
CARM (Canada)
Native CBSA Assessment and Revenue Management support, covering commercial accounting declarations, statements of account, and corrections.
IEEPA duty refunds
Identification of refund-eligible entries under shifting IEEPA actions, with PSC and CAPE filings prepared by our brokers.
Section 301 & 232
Exposure analysis, exclusion tracking, and duty mitigation across list changes, before they reach your landed cost.
ADD/CVD
Antidumping and countervailing scope screening surfaced early, not at audit time.
CPSC eFiling
PGA certificate data filed electronically with the entry, aligned to the CPSC eFiling requirements.
ISF & multi-agency filings
Importer Security Filings plus FDA, USDA, and other PGA data, validated once and filed everywhere it needs to go.
CDS (UK)
Declarations filed direct to HMRC through CDS, with postponed VAT accounting, duty deferment, and GVMS movement references handled as part of the entry, not after it.
DMS (NL)
DMS 4.0 and 4.1 declarations under the full UCC dataset, including the self-correction workflow Dutch Customs now pushes back to the declarant, plus Article 23 import VAT deferment.
AEAT (ES)
DUA filings to the Agencia Tributaria with deferred import VAT structured correctly at declaration, so import VAT lands on the periodic return instead of tying up cash at the border.
