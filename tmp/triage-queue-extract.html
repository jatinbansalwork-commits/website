The endless PR stream

PRs are piling up faster than teams can sort through and review them.

More PRs overall.

Teams and long-running agents are shipping more and longer PRs.

Teams struggle to triage & review them.

Endless PRs mean teams waste time managing queue logistics and prioritizing reviews.

More PRs become stale.
