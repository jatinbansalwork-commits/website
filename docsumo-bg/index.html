THE KLEARNOW ECOSYSTEM

KlearData powers KlearPlatform, which enables KlearServices. Each layer builds on the one below — a single coherent system, not a stack of disconnected tools.

Foundation

What's included:

  • Data ingestion & normalization
  • Tariff & duty intelligence
  • Classification & trade-program data
  • Supplier & product-level insight

Fragmented customs, shipment, classification, duty, supplier and compliance data — ingested, normalized and made queryable. This is the layer nobody else has, because nobody else files.

Operating layer

Everything in Foundation, plus:

  • Autonomous clearance & filing
  • Track & trace + drayage
  • Exposure & landed-cost analytics
  • Exception-only human review
